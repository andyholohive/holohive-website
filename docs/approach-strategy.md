September 11, 2026. Replaces the repeated UMIA / Venice / Fogo client index following the results section.

## Purpose and approved direction

Results establish evidence. This section explains the judgment behind the work: goal-led audience choices, recurring credible creator content, wider discussion and relevant paths to participation. It is an ongoing mechanism, not a numbered process, a second proof gallery, or a blanket criticism of other agencies.

Approved headline: “Build a presence that lasts beyond the post.” One supporting sentence links audience fit and credible voices with product use, launch participation and relevant relationships. One connected visual replaces the three disconnected panels and middle-panel chronology. A brief ownership line retains local strategy, execution and English reporting.

## Content boundaries

- Audience fit: research the category and relevant Korean audiences, rather than equating channel size or low coverage with opportunity.
- A story worth following: explanation, experience, perspective and updates illustrate recurring useful content. These are formats, not invented posts or a promised fixed deliverable package.
- Progress: agree the goal, shape the work around it, and use audience response to adapt. Product use, investor interest and ecosystem growth illustrate different goals; they are not a promised package or a universal acquisition funnel.
- Buyer concerns are synthesized from the call-library audits. No private quotes, proposal terms, unpublished KPIs, creator lists or operational playbooks are published.
- No fabricated data, before/after lift, guaranteed outcomes, or attribution claims.
- The wider-discussion network is conceptual. Dashed connections lead to the possible destinations Product use, Launch participation and Relevant introductions. “Depending on your goal” is visible: these are alternatives, not observed distribution, channel counts, guaranteed conversions or a fixed package. Paid creator work must not be presented as independent endorsement.
- The selected dated coverage and attribution limitation remain in the existing UMIA case page, not on the homepage. Existing case metrics are unchanged.

## Design and accessibility

- One open composition, differentiated from the results grid: audience circles connect to a forest-green creator-content block, then wider channel connections terminating in goal-led participation labels. No fourth panel, tabs, case buttons, carousel or extra CTA.
- A single oblique oval frames the audience composition. Both it and the participation origin use the same mark-inspired 45-degree oval geometry and preserved aspect ratio. They are a diagram vocabulary, not replacements for the Holo Hive logo. Goal and audience labels stay horizontal with solid backgrounds. The oval is decorative, not a fourth audience, a selection state or a Venn diagram. “Selected for your goal—not follower count” makes tailoring visible.
- The four creator formats form one compact, shallow editorial folio in the forest-green panel, inspired by the supplied HH.Index/editorial layouts. Each successive label sits fully above the next sheet. Overlap is smaller than the empty bottom padding, so wrapped or enlarged labels remain in normal flow and unobscured. Formats are unnumbered examples, not required steps. “Voices your audience trusts” connects the graphic to credibility. No invented posts, filler copy, mini icons or engagement metrics are shown.
- A ring origin has its “Wider discussion” label immediately alongside it. Three rounded paths terminate at aligned participation labels, with “Depending on your goal” directly above those alternatives. Intermediate nodes are omitted; short dashed endings preserve the conceptual, goal-dependent nature of those paths. Shared primary and secondary stroke weights distinguish meaningful paths from decorative framing and the feedback loop.
- A single return path and “Questions, participation and results shape the next move” show the ongoing nature of the work. On small screens the same message and repeat icon retain that meaning without a compressed desktop loop.
- Use established forest green, paper, white and ink tokens, without pastel gradients, fake data, decorative stock art or fabricated client content.
- A native figure and caption identify an illustrative mechanism. All SVGs are decorative; meaningful headings, audience choices, content formats and feedback stay in HTML. Native lists preserve the groups in assistive technology.
- Desktop flow becomes editorial rows on tablet and single-column groups below 740px, with vertical inter-section arrows. Principal text stays in natural flow at enlarged text sizes. Only connecting paths adapt to their routing space; oval symbols retain their proportions. No horizontal scrolling is needed to read the mechanism.
- No animation, fixed-height text clipping or interactions that move the page.
